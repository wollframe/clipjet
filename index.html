Your own clips, posted on time.

ClipJet is a small Windows desktop app for a single creator. You pick a finished vertical clip, write the caption, choose who can see it and when it should go out — ClipJet posts it to your own TikTok and YouTube Shorts accounts at that time.

Runs entirely on your own computer. No cloud, no other users, no content of anyone else.

One moment, two platforms

Each clip is prepared as a pair — one file for YouTube Shorts, one for TikTok — with its own caption. ClipJet takes both and keeps them in one queue.

You approve every post

Nothing is posted automatically on its own. A post exists only because you created it: your file, your caption, your privacy setting, your time.

Your TikTok choices, respected

Before every post ClipJet asks TikTok which privacy levels your account allows and shows only those. Comment, duet and stitch settings are yours to set per clip.

Nothing leaves your PC

Videos never pass through a server of ours — there is no server. Access tokens stay in a file on your own machine and are used only to talk to the official platform APIs.

How posting to TikTok works

  1. You sign in to TikTok once, in your system browser, through TikTok's own login page.
  2. You add a clip to the queue: file, caption, privacy level, interaction settings, date and time.
  3. At that time ClipJet uploads the file through TikTok's Content Posting API and shows you the progress and the result.
  4. You can also send the clip to your TikTok inbox as a draft instead, and finish it in the TikTok app.

ClipJet is an independent personal tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok or YouTube.
